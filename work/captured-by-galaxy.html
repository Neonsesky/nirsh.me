01 · The concept

Make every owner a creator.

The brief was a fresh campaign concept for Samsung. I built it around the Galaxy camera: a UGC-led platform, #CapturedByGalaxy, that drives sales and turns owners into the campaign’s content engine.

02 · The twist

Too on-brand to stay up.

The Instagram account I made to show the work (@capturedbygalaxy) was taken down because it resembled Samsung too closely. For a brand-fidelity exercise, that is about the strongest review possible.

03 · The strategy

Research behind the idea.

Alongside the campaign: a designed industry report on how smartphone cameras, social media and UGC feed each other, and a brand-persona study of Inglot in the Middle East.
