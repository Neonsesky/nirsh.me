Challenge

Turn a world-champion partnership into a campaign that feels unmistakably mb.io: disciplined, regulated and built to win.

What I did

Led the creative end to end: key visual, AI-assisted film edits, social and paid system, print, retail packaging, booth and merch.

Output

One master key visual rolled out across 12+ formats and every channel, all produced in-house.

01 · The idea

A perfect record, framed as perfect discipline.

29–0 is the cleanest record in MMA. For a regulated crypto platform, the parallel writes itself: discipline wins fights, and trades.

The system is restrained on purpose: black, gold, one line of type, the champion front and centre. Every format inherits from one master key visual, so the campaign reads the same on a phone screen, a poster or a hoodie.

02 · Film & AI edits

Made in-house, at film quality.

The film assets were cut and finished in-house: AI-assisted edits and generated plates were combined with designer-led grading and typography, without an external production budget.

Each cut was delivered in 9:16 for reels and stories and 4:5 for the feed.

03 · Off-screen

Print, retail, booth & merch.

The same system was carried into physical touchpoints: an event poster in gold foil style, retail packaging (“Your corner for crypto.”), a branded event booth and a merch line of caps, tees and hoodies.

Every asset was designed in-house, from master artwork to production files.

04 · What it shows

One idea, every touchpoint.

Brand discipline under pressure: a celebrity partnership, a regulated product and a dozen formats, all shipped by an in-house team without the system breaking.
